The work

Six jobs you are currently doing yourself.

  • Missed-call text-back

    Every call you cannot answer gets a text within a minute asking what they need. Most of the work you lose, you lose here — usually to whoever picked up second.

  • Quote follow-up

    Quotes get chased on day three, day seven and day fourteen without you remembering to do it. Chasing stops the moment they reply.

  • Review chasing

    A polite ask goes out once the job is signed off — to the right person, on the platform that actually matters for your trade.

  • Enquiry triage

    New enquiries sorted into work, suppliers and noise, with the urgent ones flagged before you have opened the laptop.

  • Scheduling and reminders

    Jobs booked into the diary, confirmations sent, and the day-before reminder that stops half your no-shows.

  • Price and patch watch

    We pull competitor and property data across your postcodes and send one page a month: who is charging what, where, and how it has moved since last time.

How it works

Survey the estate. Install once. Maintain it properly.

The same order you would use for anything else you put into a business — wiring, plant, a new van. Nothing gets installed before it has been measured.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Half a day, on site or on a call. We map how work actually moves through your business: who does what, where it stalls, and what the stall costs you. You keep the map whether or not you hire us.

  2. 02

    Install

    We build the agents and connect them to the tools you already use. One upfront price, agreed before anything starts. You see the number long before you see an invoice.

  3. 03

    Maintain

    Your prices change, your suppliers change, the software changes underneath you. A monthly fee keeps it running and tuned, with a named person to ring when it does not.
